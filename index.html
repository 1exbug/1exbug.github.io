ABOUT

Think like the application.

Security research is less about throwing payloads at a target and more about understanding what the application assumes is true. I map those assumptions, challenge them and turn unexpected behavior into reproducible evidence.

01

Observe

Map endpoints, roles, tokens, state transitions and trust boundaries.

02

Challenge

Test what happens when the expected sequence, identity or state changes.

03

Prove

Reduce the behavior to a clean, repeatable proof and document it responsibly.
